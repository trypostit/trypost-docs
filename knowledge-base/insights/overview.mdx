**Insights** in the sidebar is the analytics dashboard for the whole workspace. Every member of the workspace can open it. For one channel in depth, open the channel and pick **Insights**; see [Channel insights](/knowledge-base/insights/channel-insights).

## Which networks are included

Insights covers X, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, Pinterest, Bluesky and Mastodon.

LinkedIn (profile and Page), Telegram, Discord and Google Business are not included: these networks do not share analytics with third-party apps.

Posts published directly on the network, outside TryPost, count too. Each top post shows **Published via** and either TryPost or the network's logo, so you can tell them apart. After you connect a channel, TryPost imports its history in the background: "Importing account history. Older posts may appear as collection continues."

## The dashboard

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Summary** | Totals for the period, such as **Total Posts**, **Total Followers**, **Reactions**, **Comments** and **Eng. Rate**, compared with the previous period. |
| **Followers** | Followers per channel at the end of the period and how the count changed, with **At range start**, **Gained in range** and **Lost in range**. Switch the chart between **Line**, **Bar** and **Growth**. |
| **Posts** | How many posts each channel published in the period. Switch between **Bar** and **Stacked bar**. |
| **Performance** | Totals per channel for posts published in the period, compared with the previous period. Choose the metrics with **Columns**. |
| **Top 5 Posts** | Your five posts with the most reactions or comments. Switch between **Reactions** and **Comments**. Select a card to open the post's details. |

With many channels, the charts show a subset; filter by channel to see others.

Post metrics use the latest saved reading for the posts published in the range.

## Date range

Pick **7 days**, **30 days** (the default), **Month to date** or **Custom**. **Custom** offers quick picks such as **Last 3 months** or **Year to date**. Every number is compared with the previous period of the same length. Each section's subtitle shows the range and a chip such as "vs *previous range*"; hover it to read "Changes compare this period with *range*."

Days are calendar days in your time zone, and weekly charts start on your start of week. Both come from [Preferences](/knowledge-base/settings/preferences).

## Filters

Filter the dashboard by channels and by labels. If nothing matches, Insights says so and suggests another range or clearing the filters.

## Export

**Export** downloads the dashboard for the current range and filters as **CSV** or **Markdown**.

## Share as image

**Summary**, **Followers** and **Posts** have a share icon (**Share as image**) that turns the chart into a picture. In the dialog:

- **Color** picks the background color.
- **Download** saves the image.
- **Copy image** copies it to your clipboard.
- **Create post** uploads the image and opens the composer with a caption, ready to edit.

The image is marked **Made with TryPost**.

## How often data updates

- New posts are picked up every 3 hours on each channel, and every 24 hours on X.
- A post's metrics are first read about an hour after it is published, then once a day for 30 days (31 on Mastodon, so a 30-day poll gets a read after it ends). On X, where every read is billed, they are read again only 2, 3, 7, 14 and 28 days after publishing.
- Follower counts are saved once a day.

**Sync status** sums this up and shows when the channel last synced.

Follower history builds up day by day from when the channel was connected.
