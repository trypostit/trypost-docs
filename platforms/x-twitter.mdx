# X (Twitter)

TryPost publishes posts, polls and threads to X (formerly Twitter) accounts.

## Connect your account

1. In the sidebar, open **Channels** and click **Connect channel** (or use **New** > **Connect a new channel**)
2. Choose **X** and click **Connect X**
3. Authorize TryPost on X
4. On the confirmation page, check the account and click **Finish connection**

TryPost opens the new channel's **Publish** page and asks how many times a week you want to post. See [Connecting channels](/knowledge-base/channels/connecting) for the full flow.

<Tip>
  X has no way to ask for a different login. To connect a second X account, sign out of X (or switch accounts on x.com) before you click **Connect X** — or use **Switch account** on the confirmation page.
</Tip>

## Supported content types

| Type | `content_type` | Description |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| **Post** | `x_post` | Text with up to 4 media items (images, GIFs or videos, which can be combined) |
| **Poll** | `x_poll` | Question with 2 to 4 choices. No media |

## Media limits

| `content_type` | Image | Video | Duration | Format |
|----------------|-------|-------|----------|--------|
| `x_post` | 5 MB | 1 GB (Cloud cap; X allows 8 GB) | 20 minutes | JPG/PNG/GIF/WebP; video H.264 (MP4 or MOV) |
| `x_poll` | — | — | — | No media |

These are Post (`tweet_video`) limits — 8 GB / 20 minutes by default, 16 GB / 125 minutes on X Premium — not DM or Ads (`amplify_video`) limits. Official specs: [Media best practices](https://docs.x.com/x-api/media/quickstart/best-practices). Full catalog: [Media](/knowledge-base/media#x-twitter).

## Text and per-post options

- **280 characters** per post. Accounts with long posts — an X Basic, Premium or Premium+ subscription, or a verified business — get **25,000**. TryPost reads the subscription when you connect and re-checks it daily.
- **Threads** — click **Start thread** to add replies under the post, up to **24**. Each reply has its own text and up to 4 media items, and is measured against the same limit as the post. X bills each reply as a post, and only allows replies through the API to your own post. Any reply can also be a [poll](#polls).
- **AI-generated** — mark the post as made with AI (`meta.is_ai_generated`).
- **Alt text** — up to 1,000 characters per image.

## Polls

Pick **Poll** at the top of the X card to publish a poll. The post text is the question, and the poll editor below it holds the choices and how long voting stays open.

- The question is the post text, up to **280 characters**, even on an account with long posts. A poll can't be scheduled without one.
- **Choices**: **2 to 4**, each up to **25 characters** as X counts them (some emoji, such as 🐧, count as two). Each choice shows a counter and stops at 25 characters. Click **Add a choice** for a third or fourth choice. Each choice must be different; empty ones are dropped.
- **Length**: from **5 minutes to 7 days** in **Days**, **Hours** and **Minutes**. It starts at 1 day; at 7 days, **Hours** and **Minutes** are off.
- A poll can't carry images or video, not even in a draft. While the card has media or a file is uploading, **Poll** is disabled; on a poll, the media button is disabled.

**Remove poll** turns the card back into a regular post. The X preview shows the choices and the length.

Adding another network while the X card is a poll takes you back to the shared editor and turns the card back into a **Post**; pick **Poll** again when you customize the X card.

### Polls in a thread

Any post of a thread can be a poll, and one thread can hold several. The first post becomes a poll through its post type; a reply becomes a poll with the poll button in the editor toolbar (**Add a poll**). A reply poll follows the same rules: its text is the question (up to 280 characters, needed to schedule), it has 2 to 4 choices and it carries no media. While a reply has media, its poll button is disabled; **Remove poll** gives the reply its media button back. Replies that are not polls can still carry media, even under a poll.

### Poll results

Every post card in **Publish** and the post details show the poll's choices. Once it is published, each choice shows its votes and percentage, followed by the total and where voting stands: "Ends in …", then "Voting ended" once the end has passed, and "Final results" once X reports the poll closed. A reply's poll shows under it, captioned "Post 2 of the thread" (or its number).

TryPost reads the votes together with the post's other metrics, about an hour after publishing and then 2, 3, 7, 14 and 28 days later, so the numbers are not live. Until the first read, the poll shows its choices with 0 votes.

## Links in posts

X charges much more to deliver a post that carries a link, and shows it to fewer people. To keep a post out of that bracket, TryPost publishes the X version of a post with its links written non-clickable: `https://www.example.com/pricing` goes out as `example(.)com/pricing`.

Only the X version changes. The same post published to your other networks keeps its links exactly as you typed them, and nothing in your draft is edited — the rewrite happens on the way out.

The editor previews the X post as it will be published and counts characters against that same text, so the limit reflects what X receives. A reader reaches the page by copying the address and putting the dots back — the trade for keeping the post's reach.

<Note>
  Self-hosting TryPost? Links go out untouched there by default, since you publish through your own X app and pay your own X bill — see [link defusing on X](/self-hosting/configuration#link-defusing-on-x).
</Note>

## Insights

X posts and followers appear in [Insights](/knowledge-base/insights/overview). Because X bills every read, TryPost checks X for new posts once a day, and posts made directly on X are imported into **Sent** only from the last 30 days. A poll made directly on X is imported as a **Poll**, with its choices and length.

## OAuth scopes

`tweet.read`, `tweet.write`, `users.read`, `media.write`, `offline.access`

`offline.access` is required so TryPost can refresh the access token in the background — without it, you'd have to reauthorize regularly.

## Self-hosting setup

<Accordion title="Configure X API credentials">
  1. Go to [X Developer Portal](https://developer.twitter.com/) and create a project/app
  2. Enable **OAuth 2.0** in your app settings
  3. On the User authentication settings page, enable the scopes listed above
  4. Set the redirect URL to `{APP_URL}/accounts/x/callback`
  5. Add to your `.env`:

  ```env
  X_CLIENT_ID=your_client_id
  X_CLIENT_SECRET=your_client_secret
  X_CLIENT_REDIRECT="${APP_URL}/accounts/x/callback"
  ```

  How often TryPost reads X is set by `X_PUBLICATION_DISCOVERY_INTERVAL_HOURS` and `X_EXTERNAL_POSTS_IMPORT_DAYS` — see [Importing posts made outside TryPost](/self-hosting/configuration#importing-posts-made-outside-trypost).

  Poll votes ride on the metrics read TryPost already makes. When a thread has polls on its replies, that read asks for the post and those replies in one request, and X bills each extra post it returns.

  Hide X with `X_ENABLED=false`.
</Accordion>
