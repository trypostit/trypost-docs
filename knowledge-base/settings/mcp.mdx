MCP (Model Context Protocol) lets an AI assistant work in your workspace: create and schedule posts, read metrics, manage ideas, labels and more. Open **Settings → MCP** to connect one.

Every member of the workspace can connect their own assistant. It acts as you, with your permissions: if your posts need approval, posts created through your assistant need approval too. See [Members and permissions](/knowledge-base/settings/members-and-permissions).

## Connect an assistant

**Settings → MCP** has two tabs: **Connected apps** and **Connect an app**. On **Connect an app**, click **Connect Claude**, **Connect ChatGPT**, or **Connect** under **Other apps** (any app that supports MCP). A dialog, **Connect TryPost to** *app*, lists the steps for that app:

- **Claude** — the dialog shows the **Name** (`TryPost`) and **Server URL**, each with **Copy**. Click **Open Claude** to go straight to Claude's custom connector form, paste the URL into the connector's URL field, then click **Add** and **Connect**. See [Claude](/ai/claude).
- **ChatGPT** — there is nothing to paste. Click **Open ChatGPT** to open the TryPost app in ChatGPT and click **Connect** there. See [ChatGPT](/ai/chatgpt).
- **Other apps** — the dialog, **Connect TryPost to another app**, shows the **Name**, **Server URL** and a **Config** snippet to paste into **Cursor**, **VS Code**, **Claude Code** or any other client that reads an `mcpServers` config. Add a custom MCP server in your app and paste the name and URL, or the config. The snippet names the server `TryPost`; the setup guides use `trypost`. If you paste the snippet, use `TryPost` in commands such as `codex mcp login TryPost`.

On TryPost Cloud the server URL is:

```
https://app.trypost.it/mcp/trypost
```

Then log in to TryPost and pick your workspace: the first time the assistant connects, your browser opens **Authorize** *assistant name*. Pick the **Workspace** and click **Authorize**. This connection will only access the selected workspace.

While you do this, the dialog shows "Waiting for your AI to connect…". As soon as the assistant is connected, it closes and the app appears under **Connected apps**.

To use the assistant with another workspace, connect it again and pick that workspace.

Step-by-step guides for each assistant, and the list of every tool, are in [Build with AI](/ai/introduction).

<Note>
  Self-hosting TryPost? Use `{APP_URL}/mcp/trypost` as the server URL. To connect ChatGPT to your own instance, see [ChatGPT](/ai/chatgpt).
</Note>

## Connected apps

**Connected apps** lists the assistants you've signed in with for this workspace, with when each was **Last used**. Each person sees only their own. With none yet, it shows **No connected apps**.

To remove one, click **Disconnect** and type `disconnect` to confirm. This signs the app out of TryPost: it will need to reconnect before it can use MCP again.

Removing a member from the workspace also disconnects their apps.

## What an assistant can and cannot do

- It can do what the [tools](/ai/tools-reference) allow, within your permissions in the workspace.
- TryPost's AI writing features (the composer's and idea editor's AI assistant, **Generate ideas**, alt text) are not available over MCP; your assistant writes the text itself.
- On TryPost Cloud, the account needs an active subscription.
- API keys don't work for MCP, and MCP connections don't work for the REST API. For scripts and CI, use [API keys](/knowledge-base/settings/api-keys).
