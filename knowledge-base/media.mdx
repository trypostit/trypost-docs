Media is added and edited inside the [composer](/knowledge-base/publish/composer). There is no separate media library: each post keeps its own copy of its files, and deleting a post deletes its media.

## Supported files

| Type | Formats |
| --- | --- |
| Image | JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP. HEIC photos from iPhones are converted to JPEG. |
| Video | MP4, MOV |
| Document | PDF (LinkedIn only) |

Maximum size per file on TryPost Cloud:

| Type | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Image | 10 MB, and no more pixels than 8192 × 8192 (about 67 megapixels) |
| Video | 1 GB |
| PDF | 100 MB |

Many networks accept less than that. The composer checks every file against the networks and post types you selected; see [Limits by network](#limits-by-network).

<Note>
  Self-hosting TryPost? The per-file limits are set with `MEDIA_IMAGE_MAX_SIZE_MB`, `MEDIA_VIDEO_MAX_SIZE_MB` and `MEDIA_DOCUMENT_MAX_SIZE_MB`, and HEIC conversion needs ImageMagick with HEIC support. See [Configuration](/self-hosting/configuration).
</Note>

## Add media

- **Upload from computer**, drag and drop files onto the composer, or paste them.
- **More media sources** opens the other sources available in your workspace: **Unsplash** (free photos), **Google Drive**, **Google Photos** and **Canva**. Only the sources that are set up appear in the menu.

With Canva, pick a size preset (**Square 1:1**, **Portrait 4:5**, **Portrait 3:4**, **Landscape 1.91:1**, **Story 9:16**) to start a design. When you are done it comes back to the post. Later, **Edit in Canva** reopens the design and brings the new version back.

Files that fail to upload show **Retry** and **Remove**. You cannot save the post until every upload succeeds or is removed.

### Reorder

Drag a file by its handle to change the order, or focus it and press the left and right arrow keys. The order in the composer is the order on the network, which matters for carousels and multi-image posts.

## Edit Media

Click **Edit** on a file to open **Edit Media**: "Crop, adjust, describe and tag the images of this post." The tabs depend on the file and the post type.

### Crop

- **Crop & Transform**: **Freeform**, **Original**, and portrait, square and landscape presets.
- **Rotate & Flip**: rotate left or right, flip horizontally or vertically.
- **Straighten** with a slider.

### Appearance

- **Filters**: **Original**, **Vivid**, **Mono**, **Sepia**, with **Filter intensity**.
- **Adjust**: **Brightness**, **Contrast**, **Saturation**, **Warmth**.

**Apply** saves the edited images to the post. The original stays selected if the edit cannot be uploaded.

### Alt text

**Alt Text** adds a description for people with visual impairments. It is also shown if the image fails to load.

**Generate with AI** writes a description of the image for you, in your interface language. It works for images only and is part of the web app; it is not available through the API or MCP.

TikTok, YouTube, Telegram and Google Business do not take alt text. The other networks cap its length (for example 500 characters on Pinterest and 1,000 on X).

### Tag People

On Instagram feed posts, **Tag People** lets you click the image where a person appears and type their Instagram username. Up to 20 tags per image. In the API and MCP they are the media item's `meta.user_tags`, also when attaching media to an existing post. "Instagram API limitations prevent searching for usernames and tagging private users."

### Thumbnail

For Instagram feed videos and Reels, TikTok videos and Pinterest video pins, **Thumbnail** lets you drag a slider to pick the frame shown as the video's cover.

## What TryPost does to your files

- Images you edit are saved as new images; the edits do not touch your originals.
- Instagram and Facebook Story images are fitted onto a vertical 9:16 canvas.
- Some networks get a resized copy of an image when it is above their limit (for example, Bluesky images are re-encoded under Bluesky's 2 MB limit).
- Videos are sent as you uploaded them. TryPost does not convert them.

## Limits by network

The composer shows a warning on a channel when its media does not fit, and links to the matching section below. The numbers are TryPost Cloud's: a network's own limit, capped at the per-file limits above. The live values for every post type are in [List content types](/api-reference/platform/list-content-types).

Thread replies on X, Bluesky and Mastodon follow the same rules as a post on that network, per reply.

### Instagram

| Post type | Media |
| --- | --- |
| **Feed Post** | Up to 10 files: images up to 8 MB, videos up to 100 MB and 60 seconds |
| **Reel** | 1 video, up to 300 MB and 15 minutes |
| **Story** | 1 image (8 MB) or 1 video (100 MB, 60 seconds). Stories have no caption. |

No GIFs. Alt text up to 1,000 characters. **Tag People** on feed posts. See [Instagram](/platforms/instagram).

### Facebook

| Post type | Media |
| --- | --- |
| **Post** | Up to 10 images (4 MB each) **or** 1 video (1 GB, 240 minutes) |
| **Reel** | 1 video, up to 1 GB and 90 seconds |
| **Story** | 1 image (10 MB) or 1 video (1 GB, 60 seconds). Stories have no caption. |

No GIFs. Alt text up to 1,000 characters. See [Facebook](/platforms/facebook).

### Threads

| Post type | Media |
| --- | --- |
| **Thread** | Up to 10 files: images up to 8 MB, videos up to 1 GB and 5 minutes |
| **Ghost post** | Text only, no media |

No GIFs. Alt text up to 1,000 characters. See [Threads](/platforms/threads).

### X (Twitter)

| Post type | Media |
| --- | --- |
| **Post** | Up to 4 files: images up to 5 MB, videos up to 1 GB and 20 minutes |
| **Poll** | No media |

GIFs are accepted. Alt text up to 1,000 characters. A reply that is a poll carries no media either. See [X (Twitter)](/platforms/x-twitter).

### LinkedIn

| Post type | Media |
| --- | --- |
| **Post** (profile and Page) | Up to 10 images (5 MB each), **or** 1 video (1 GB, 10 minutes), **or** 1 PDF (100 MB) on its own |

Images, a video and a PDF cannot be mixed. No GIFs. A PDF post can have a **Document title**. Alt text up to 4,086 characters. See [LinkedIn](/platforms/linkedin).

### TikTok

| Post type | Media |
| --- | --- |
| **Video** | 1 video, up to 1 GB and 10 minutes (TikTok may set a lower limit for your account) |
| **Photo carousel** | 1 to 35 images, up to 10 MB each |

The attached media decides the type: photos and a video never mix. No GIFs and no alt text. See [TikTok](/platforms/tiktok).

### YouTube

| Post type | Media |
| --- | --- |
| **Short** | 1 video, up to 1 GB and 3 minutes |

A video is required. No images and no alt text. See [YouTube](/platforms/youtube).

### Pinterest

| Post type | Media |
| --- | --- |
| **Photo** | 1 image, up to 10 MB |
| **Video** | 1 video, up to 1 GB and 15 minutes |
| **Carousel** | 2 to 5 images, up to 10 MB each |

No GIFs. Alt text up to 500 characters. See [Pinterest](/platforms/pinterest).

### Bluesky

| Post type | Media |
| --- | --- |
| **Post** | Up to 4 images (10 MB each) **or** 1 video (300 MB, 10 minutes) |

Images and a video cannot be mixed. GIFs are accepted. Images are re-encoded under Bluesky's 2 MB limit when published; a GIF over 2 MB fails. Alt text up to 2,000 characters. See [Bluesky](/platforms/bluesky).

### Mastodon

| Post type | Media |
| --- | --- |
| **Post** | Up to 4 images (10 MB each) **or** 1 video (40 MB) |

GIFs are accepted. Alt text up to 1,500 characters. See [Mastodon](/platforms/mastodon).

### Discord

| Post type | Media |
| --- | --- |
| **Message** | Up to 10 files: images up to 10 MB, videos up to 20 MB |

GIFs are accepted. Alt text up to 1,024 characters. See [Discord](/platforms/discord).

### Telegram

| Post type | Media |
| --- | --- |
| **Post** | Up to 10 files: images up to 5 MB, videos up to 20 MB, sent as an album |

GIFs are accepted. No alt text. See [Telegram](/platforms/telegram).

### Google Business Profile

| Post type | Media |
| --- | --- |
| **Post** | 1 image, at least 250 px on its short side |

No videos, no GIFs and no alt text. See [Google Business](/platforms/google-business).
