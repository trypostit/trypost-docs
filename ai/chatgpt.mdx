[ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com) connects to TryPost through the TryPost app in ChatGPT, so you can manage social media scheduling with natural language in chat. There is nothing to paste: you open the app, click Connect and sign in to TryPost.

## Prerequisites

- A [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com) account
- A TryPost Cloud account with an active subscription ([plans and billing](/knowledge-base/settings/plans-and-billing))

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the connect dialog">
    In TryPost, open **Settings → MCP**, go to the **Connect an app** tab and click **Connect ChatGPT**. The **Connect TryPost to ChatGPT** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open TryPost in ChatGPT">
    Click **Open ChatGPT**. ChatGPT opens on the TryPost app. Click **Connect** there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and pick a workspace">
    Log in to TryPost when asked, pick the **Workspace** this connection may use and click **Authorize**. To use another workspace, connect again and pick it.

    Back in TryPost, the dialog shows "Waiting for your AI to connect…" and closes as soon as ChatGPT is connected. ChatGPT then appears under **Connected apps**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use TryPost in a conversation">
    Start a new chat in ChatGPT and ask:

    ```
    List all my scheduled posts
    ```

    ChatGPT will call the TryPost tools to fetch your posts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To disconnect ChatGPT, see [Connected apps](/knowledge-base/settings/mcp#connected-apps).

<Accordion title="Self-hosting TryPost? Add it as a developer-mode app">
  The TryPost app in ChatGPT connects to TryPost Cloud. To connect ChatGPT to your own instance, add it as a developer-mode app instead. This needs a ChatGPT **Pro, Plus, Business, Enterprise, or Education** account on the **web** ([Developer mode](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/developer-mode) is not available on Free and not guaranteed on mobile).

  ChatGPT only connects to remote HTTPS MCP servers (from OpenAI's infrastructure), so your instance must be publicly reachable over HTTPS. Local `stdio` MCP servers are not supported.

  1. In ChatGPT on the web, open **Settings → Security and login** and turn on **Developer mode**. If that toggle is missing, try **Settings → Apps → Advanced settings**. On Business / Enterprise / Education workspaces, an admin may need to allow Developer mode first.
  2. Open the connectors flow ([chatgpt.com/plugins#settings/Connectors?create-connector=true&redirectAfter=%2Fplugins](https://chatgpt.com/plugins#settings/Connectors?create-connector=true&redirectAfter=%2Fplugins)), or use **Settings → Plugins** / **Settings → Apps** and click **+**. Fill in:

     | Field | Value |
     |-------|-------|
     | **Name** | `TryPost` |
     | **Description** | `Schedule and publish social posts with TryPost` |
     | **MCP server URL** | `{APP_URL}/mcp/trypost` |

     For authentication, use **OAuth**. Leave Client ID / Secret blank if shown — TryPost supports Dynamic Client Registration and discovers OAuth from the URL.
  3. Create the connection. ChatGPT may let you finish creating it without signing in; it then asks you to sign in inside the conversation the first time you use TryPost, and again whenever the sign-in expires. Pick the **Workspace** and click **Authorize**.
  4. In a new chat, choose **Developer mode** from the composer **+** menu and enable the **TryPost** app for that conversation.

  Write actions (create, update, publish, delete) require confirmation by default in Developer mode; tools annotated as read-only skip it. After you upgrade TryPost, open the app under **Settings → Plugins** (or **Apps**), click **Refresh**, and start a new conversation so ChatGPT picks up updated tools.

  Listing your own instance in the ChatGPT apps directory needs `OPENAI_APPS_CHALLENGE` — see [configuration](/self-hosting/configuration).
</Accordion>
