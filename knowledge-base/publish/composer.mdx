The composer is where you write posts. Open it with:

- **New** → **Post** in the sidebar;
- **New post** on the [Publish](/knowledge-base/publish/overview) page;
- **+ New** on a free [queue slot](/knowledge-base/publish/queue), or a click on the calendar, which opens it at that time.

## Write the post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the channels">
    Select one or more connected channels. Saving creates one post per channel. **Last used** selects the channels of the last post you saved in this browser. The channel search matches every word you type and ignores accents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write and add media">
    Write the text in the shared editor and add images, videos or a PDF. See [Media](/knowledge-base/media).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when">
    Pick a scheduling option (see [When it publishes](#when-it-publishes)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize for each network">
    With more than one network selected, click **Customize for each network** to review each one. With a single network you are already there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Schedule posts**, **Add to queue** or **Publish posts**, or **Save draft(s)** at any point.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Tick **Create another** to keep the composer open for the next post.

### Unsaved changes

If you changed a post and try to leave without saving (close the composer, press <kbd>Esc</kbd>, click outside it, follow a link, switch workspace or press the browser's Back), TryPost asks "Discard changes?" **Discard** drops your changes; **Cancel** keeps you editing. Reloading the page shows your browser's own prompt instead. A post you opened and did not change closes without asking.

When you open the composer from a page of the app (a list, the calendar, a channel tab), saving or closing takes you back to that page, and the browser's Back then reaches the page before it.

### Step 1: one shared editor

The first step is one editor for every selected network. Your text, media and links go to all of them.

### Step 2: one card per network

**Customize for each network** opens one card per network, in the same order as your channel list. Each card starts as a copy of the shared text, and you can change the text, media, post type and network settings for that network only.

<Warning>
  Going back to step 1 discards every per-network change, including post types and settings. TryPost asks first: "You may lose your changes."
</Warning>

Pinterest, Discord and TikTok settings (board, channel, privacy) stay per account. Every other setting applies to all accounts of that network.

### Post types and network settings

Each network offers its own post types, such as **Feed Post**, **Reel** and **Story** on Instagram, or **Post**, **Reel** and **Story** on Facebook. On Pinterest and TikTok there is no post-type choice: the attached media decides it (a video makes a video pin or TikTok video, several images a Pinterest carousel, images alone a TikTok photo carousel). Some networks have extra settings:

| Network | Settings |
| --- | --- |
| TikTok | **Visibility** (required), **Allow comments**, **Allow Duet**, **Allow Stitch**, **Auto add music**, **Disclose video content**, **Your brand**, **Branded content** |
| Pinterest | Board (required), **Title**, **Destination link** |
| Discord | **Channel** (required), **Mentions**, **Embeds** |
| YouTube | **Title**, **Description**, **Category**, **Visibility**, **License**, **Notify subscribers**, **Allow embedding**, **Made for kids** |
| Google Business | **Post type** (What's New, Event, Offer), **Button** |
| Mastodon | **Content warning** |
| Threads | **Topic**, and the **Ghost post** type (text only, archived after 24 hours) |
| X | The **Poll** type: 2 to 4 choices and how long voting stays open, no media. See [Polls](/platforms/x-twitter#polls) |
| LinkedIn | **Document title** for PDF posts, and the **Add comment** button for one comment under the post. See [Comment](/platforms/linkedin#comment) |
| Instagram | **Collaborators** (feed posts and Reels); on Reels also **Music** (accounts connected through Facebook), **Trial reel** and **Share to feed** |

Instagram, TikTok videos, X and YouTube also offer the **AI-generated** switch: "Tell viewers your content was generated or edited with AI."

Each network has its own page under **Platforms**, for example [TikTok](/platforms/tiktok) and [Google Business](/platforms/google-business). Instagram's **Music**, **Collaborators** and **Trial reel** are explained on [Instagram](/platforms/instagram#music).

**YouTube:** without a **Title**, the first non-empty line of your text becomes the title (up to 100 characters, without `<` or `>`). A post with no title and no text cannot be scheduled or published on YouTube.

## Character limits

The composer counts characters for each channel and warns before you save.

- Limits are per account. An X account with long posts (a Basic, Premium or Premium+ subscription, or a verified business) gets 25,000 characters instead of 280.
- No post can be longer than 25,000 characters, not even a draft.
- Every character counts once, emoji included.
- Mastodon's content warning counts toward its 500 characters.
- An X poll's question (the post text) is capped at 280 characters, even with long posts.
- Instagram allows up to 5 hashtags. The counter shows how many hashtags you have left.
- Instagram and Facebook Stories send no caption, so their text is not measured.

The network limits are checked when you schedule or publish, not when you save a draft. X poll choices are the exception: more than 4, a repeated one or one over 25 characters is refused even on a draft. Each network's limit is on its page in the **Platforms** section of the docs, for example [X (Twitter)](/platforms/x-twitter) or [Instagram](/platforms/instagram).

<Note>
  On TryPost Cloud, links in the X version of a post may be shown without being clickable. See [X (Twitter)](/platforms/x-twitter).
</Note>

## Threads of posts

On X, Bluesky and Mastodon you can publish a thread:

- **Start thread** adds a reply under the first post; **Add to thread** adds another.
- A thread has up to 24 replies.
- Each reply has its own text and media, and needs one of the two (a poll reply needs text and has no media). Its media appears under that reply only.
- Each reply is measured against the account's character limit.
- On X, a reply can be a poll: click the poll button (**Add a poll**) in the toolbar. See [Polls in a thread](/platforms/x-twitter#polls-in-a-thread).
- Press <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> to start the next post of the thread, and <kbd>Backspace</kbd> in an empty one to remove it. In a reply, once the thread has 24 replies, <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> breaks the line instead.

On LinkedIn, **Add comment** in the card's toolbar adds one text-only comment, published under the post from the same profile or page. See [Comment](/platforms/linkedin#comment).

Other networks show "Threads of posts are not available on this network."

## Link previews

When your text has a link, the composer shows a link preview card.

- The × (**Remove link preview**) publishes the post without the card. Facebook posts, Bluesky and LinkedIn honour it.
- Threads always shows a card for the first link, so it has no ×.
- **Replace link preview with media** adds the card's image to the post as media.

## When it publishes

| Option | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Now** | "Publish your post right away." |
| **Next available** | "Add after the posts already in the queue." Takes the first free queue slot. |
| **Prioritize** | "Put this post at the top of the queue." |
| **Set date and time** | "Choose a specific time to publish." |

The queue options need posting times on every selected channel. If one has none, the composer says which ("No posting times:" followed by the channel names) and links to **Manage posting times**. See [Queue](/knowledge-base/publish/queue).

The option the composer starts with is your **Default posting action** in [Preferences](/knowledge-base/settings/preferences).

### Time zone

Times in the composer are shown in the composer zone, labelled next to the time:

- if every selected channel has the same time zone, that zone;
- otherwise, your own time zone from Preferences.

With exactly one channel selected, the date and time picker offers that channel's posting times for the day, and greys out the ones another post already holds.

## Members who need approval

If your access is **Needs approval**, the save button reads **Save and request approval**. The post waits in [Approvals](/knowledge-base/publish/approvals) instead of being scheduled or published.

## AI Assistant

The **AI Assistant** helps you write:

- **Generate** writes a post from a prompt ("What do you want to write about?").
- **Rephrase**, **Shorten**, **Expand**, **More casual** and **More formal** rewrite your current text.

Then **Insert**, **Replace** your text, or **Regenerate**. The suggestion is tailored to the channel's character limit. New text is written in your interface language; rewrites keep the language of your text.

The assistant is part of the web app only; it is not available through the API or MCP.

<Note>
  Self-hosting TryPost? The assistant needs an AI provider key. See [AI](/self-hosting/ai).
</Note>

## Also in the composer

- **Templates** opens your [templates](/knowledge-base/create/templates); picking one inserts its text.
- Insert a [signature](/knowledge-base/settings/signatures) from the signature picker.
- Add [labels](/knowledge-base/settings/labels) to the post.
- Add emoji from the emoji picker in the toolbar.
- **Expand dialog** opens the composer full screen; **Exit full screen** brings it back.
- With no channel picked, **Last used** selects the channels you used last time; **Select all** picks every channel (**Deselect all** clears them).
- A file you remove stays under **Suggested media** until you close the composer, so you can add it back with one click.
- An empty editor says "Start writing or get inspired with **Templates**"; the link opens your templates.
- **Post previews** show each network's version. "Previews are an approximation. Each network decides the final look of your post."
- If you close the composer without saving, the next time you open it TryPost asks **Resume unfinished post?** Choose **Resume post** or **Discard**.
