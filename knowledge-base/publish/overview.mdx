**Publish** is where your posts live. Open it from the sidebar for **All channels**, or open one channel in the **Channels** section and pick **Publish** to see only that channel.

**Publish** opens the view you used last in this browser: **List**, **Calendar** or **Board**. The first time, it opens the list.

Each post belongs to one channel. When you save a post for several channels in the [composer](/knowledge-base/publish/composer), TryPost creates one post per channel, so each can be edited, moved or deleted on its own.

## Tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Queue** | Scheduled posts, and posts being published right now, in time order. |
| **Approvals** | Posts waiting for approval. See [Approvals](/knowledge-base/publish/approvals). |
| **Drafts** | Drafts. |
| **Sent** | Published and failed posts. |

Lists load more posts as you scroll.

Published posts are kept for 2 years, then deleted from TryPost with their media. They stay on the network.

<Note>
  Self-hosting TryPost? Change this with `POST_HISTORY_RETENTION_DAYS`. See [Configuration](/self-hosting/configuration).
</Note>

Sent posts show their latest metrics right on the card, with when they were last refreshed ("Refreshed 2 hours ago"). Use the arrows to page through the metrics, and **See post insights** for everything the network reports. Until the first collection a post shows "Metrics have not been collected yet." Networks without analytics (LinkedIn, Telegram, Discord, Google Business) show none; see [Insights](/knowledge-base/insights/overview).

A poll on X, Mastodon or Threads also shows its votes per choice; see Poll results on [X](/platforms/x-twitter#poll-results), [Mastodon](/platforms/mastodon#poll-results) and [Threads](/platforms/threads#poll-results).

**Sent** also shows posts you published directly on the network, outside TryPost. They are marked "This was published directly from Instagram" (or the channel's network).

## List, Calendar and Board

Switch between **List**, **Calendar** and **Board**. The tabs above belong to the list.

- The calendar has **3 days**, **Week** and **Month** views, and a **Status** filter: **All posts**, **Drafts**, **Scheduled** or **Sent**.
- **Undated drafts** lists drafts without a date.
- Clicking a time on the calendar opens the composer at that time.
- Instagram channels have one more view, **Grid**: an approximation of your profile grid.

### Board

**Board** shows your posts as columns of cards. Pick how to split them with **Group by**:

| Group by | Columns |
| --- | --- |
| **Status** | **Draft**, **Approvals**, **Scheduled**, **Published** and **Failed**. |
| **Label** | One column per label, then **Untagged**. A post with two labels shows in both columns. |
| **Channel** | One column per channel, in the sidebar's order. Only on **All channels**. |

Each column shows how many posts it holds and loads more cards as you scroll. An empty column says "Nothing here". If your filters leave no column, the board says "Nothing matches these filters".

Click a card to open its **Post Details**. The card's menu has the same actions as on the list, except reordering the queue. Approvers can **Approve**, **Schedule**, **Add to queue** or **Reject** a pending post right on its card; see [Approvals](/knowledge-base/publish/approvals).

## Filters and time zone

- **Labels** and **Channels** filter the list, the calendar and the board. **Untagged** shows posts without labels. See [Labels](/knowledge-base/settings/labels).
- **Time zone** sets the zone the page shows times in. It starts as your own time zone from [Preferences](/knowledge-base/settings/preferences). Changing it only changes what you see; each channel still publishes on its own schedule.
- The filters and the time zone stay set when you switch between **List**, **Calendar** and **Board**.

## Post actions

Each post card has buttons and a menu. What you see depends on the post's status and your permissions:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit** | Open the post in the composer. |
| **Publish Now** | Publish a draft right away. |
| **Add to queue** | "Add to the channel's next free posting time." On a channel without posting times it opens "You haven't set up this channel's queue yet" with **Set posting times** and **Not now**; a member who is not an admin is told that a workspace admin needs to set them. |
| **Move to top**, **Move up**, **Move down** | Reorder queued posts. See [Queue](/knowledge-base/publish/queue). |
| **Make Recurring** / **Edit Recurrence** | Repeat a scheduled post. See [Recurring posts](/knowledge-base/publish/recurring-posts). |
| **Duplicate Post** | Create a draft copy of the post right away and open it in the composer. |
| **Move to Drafts** | Unschedule a post. |
| **Edit labels** | Change the post's labels. |
| **Delete Post** | Delete the post and its media from TryPost. Members who need approval can delete only posts they wrote, or a request they made while it is still pending. |
| **Open post on Instagram** (or the channel's network) | Open the live post. |
| **Share link in a post** | Open a new post with the link to the live post. |
| **Copy link** | Copy the link to the live post. |
| **See post insights** | Open the post's metrics. See [Channel insights](/knowledge-base/insights/channel-insights). |
| **Post Details** | Show the full post, its channels, who created it and, once sent, its metrics. |

Posts that are **Publishing**, **Published** or **Failed** cannot be edited or deleted. To try again, duplicate the post. A post that was deleted on the network can be deleted from TryPost. Deleting a post never removes it from the network.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Saved, not scheduled. |
| **Pending** | Waiting for an approver. On the calendar, its tooltip reads "Pending approval". |
| **Scheduled** | Will publish at its time. |
| **Publishing** | Being sent to the network now. |
| **Retrying** | The network had a temporary problem; TryPost tries again at the time shown. |
| **Published** | Live on the network. |
| **Failed** | Could not be published. |
| **Deleted** | Published, then deleted on the network. |

### Posts deleted on the network

When someone deletes a published post on Facebook, Instagram, Threads, YouTube, X, Bluesky, Mastodon or Pinterest, TryPost finds out the next time it [reads the post's metrics](/knowledge-base/insights/channel-insights#how-often-data-updates). The post shows **Deleted**, with a trash mark on the calendar. Its badge opens "Deleted on Instagram" (or the channel's network): "It was removed after it was published, so its link and insights are no longer available." The post loses **Open post on Instagram**, **Share link in a post** and **Copy link**, and you can delete it from TryPost. If the network lists the post again, it goes back to **Published**.

TikTok, LinkedIn, Google Business, Telegram and Discord posts are not checked, so they stay **Published**.

### Cover not applied

A published YouTube Short with a cover image of its own can show **Warning**. YouTube refused the cover, but the video is live and the post stays **Published**. Click **Warning** to see **Cover not applied** and why. See [YouTube covers](/platforms/youtube#cover).

### Why a post failed

A failed post shows **Why it failed** with a plain reason and the **Message from the network**. The reasons are:

- The network didn't accept the media format, size or length. See [Media](/knowledge-base/media).
- The network is limiting how often this channel can post.
- The channel doesn't have permission to publish this post.
- The network rejected the content under its posting rules.
- The network had an error, was unavailable, or took too long to respond.
- The channel's connection expired. Reconnect it in [Channel settings](/knowledge-base/channels/settings).
- Publishing stopped before the network confirmed the post.
- We couldn't publish this post. (the network gave no reason TryPost recognizes)

A post that is longer than the network allows fails with a message such as "This post has 312 characters, but X allows 280."

## Notes

Click **Show notes** at the bottom of a post card to talk with your team about it. The notes open inside the card, on every tab, and **Hide notes** closes them. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to send and <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> for a new line. A link to a note in an email opens that post's notes and highlights the note. You can **Edit** or **Delete** your own notes; edited notes are marked "edited". Scroll up to load older notes.

Type `@` to mention someone: the list starts with **@all** ("Everyone on this post"), then your teammates. Hover over a mention to see that person's local time. To react, click the smiley (**Add reaction**) and pick an emoji, or click an existing reaction to add or take back yours. New notes and reactions show up for everyone who has the post open.

Each person you mention gets an email. A new note also emails the post's other participants: its author, the member who asked for approval and everyone who already wrote a note on it. Nobody else is emailed, the note's author never is, and editing a note only emails the people the edit adds as mentions. Reactions send no email. On a post pending approval, only the approvers and the member who asked see its notes, so only they can be mentioned. Each person can turn these emails off with **New note on a post** in [Notifications](/knowledge-base/settings/notifications).

## Channel page extras

Admins see a gear in the header: **Manage channels** on **All channels** opens [Settings → Channels](/knowledge-base/channels/settings), and **Channel settings** on a channel opens that channel's settings.

When you open **Publish** for a single channel, the header also shows:

- the posting goal, "2/5 posts sent this week", with **Sent**, **Scheduled** and **To do**. The week follows your start of week in [Preferences](/knowledge-base/settings/preferences).
- **Show posting times** and **Manage posting times** for the channel's [queue](/knowledge-base/publish/queue).

## Empty workspace

A workspace without channels shows "Welcome to TryPost 👋" with a button to connect your first channel. See [Connecting a channel](/knowledge-base/channels/connecting).
