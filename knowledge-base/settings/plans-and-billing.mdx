<Note>
  This page is about TryPost Cloud. A self-hosted TryPost has no plans or billing: every feature is available and there is no workspace limit.
</Note>

## Plans

Both plans have the same features. The difference is how many workspaces you get, and you can switch plans whenever you like.

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | Workspaces |
|------|---------|--------|------------|
| **Socials** — best for creators and small brands | $19/month | $190/year | One workspace |
| **Workspaces** — best for agencies and larger businesses | $99/month | $990/year | Unlimited workspaces |

Yearly billing gives you 2 months free.

A [workspace](/knowledge-base/settings/workspaces) is one brand or client, kept separate from the rest: its own social accounts, signatures, labels, analytics, member permissions and MCP connection.

### Everything included

On both plans:

- All social networks
- Unlimited social accounts, even several from the same network
- Unlimited members
- Calendar and queue
- The AI assistant in the composer
- [MCP](/knowledge-base/settings/mcp): post from Claude, ChatGPT or other assistants
- [Repurpose](/knowledge-base/repurpose)
- [Insights](/knowledge-base/insights/overview)

There are no AI credits or usage limits.

## Start your subscription

After you sign up, the welcome steps end with **Plan**: **Activate your account**, on the **Socials** plan at $19/month. Click **Activate my account** to finish at Stripe Checkout, which asks for a card. Checkout has a field for a promotion code.

The welcome step is Socials, monthly only. Once subscribed, you can switch to yearly or to **Workspaces** with **Change plan**.

Until the owner finishes checkout, members of the account see **Waiting for the account owner**.

### Offers

An offer link (an address with `?offer=` from a TryPost campaign) changes the plan step: it shows the offer's price, such as a discount for your first months or **Free** for a free trial. A trial that needs no card starts right away, without Stripe Checkout; one that needs a card goes through Stripe Checkout like a normal subscription. Each offer works once per account, and only while the account has no active subscription. Offers apply to the Socials monthly price. When an offer already gives a discount, Checkout has no promotion code field.

## The Billing page

Only the account owner can manage billing. Open it from **Settings → Billing**, or **Plans and billing** in the workspace menu. It has two tabs.

**Plan**:

- **Current plan** — your plan and price with its status: **Active**, **Free trial**, **Cancelled** or **Past due**. Below it, **Trial until** *date* during a trial, or **Cancelled, ends on** *date* after you cancel. A discount from an offer or promotion code shows how much it takes off: on your next payment, until a date, or on every payment.
- **Payment methods** — your cards, with **Default** on the one Stripe charges. **Manage on Stripe** opens Stripe to update your card or billing details, or to cancel.

**Invoices** lists your past invoices by **Date**, **Amount** and **Status**. Click **Download invoice** for the PDF.

If a payment fails, the status reads **Past due**. A **Payment past due** notice also appears at the bottom of the sidebar, with **Update payment** to fix your card on Stripe.

## Change plan

Click **Change plan** to upgrade or downgrade, or to switch between monthly and yearly. The change applies to your current subscription right away.

To move from Workspaces to Socials you can have only one workspace: with more, TryPost asks you to delete the extras before switching. See [Delete a workspace](/knowledge-base/settings/workspaces#delete-a-workspace).

## Cancel

Cancel from **Manage on Stripe**. The Billing page then shows **Cancelled, ends on** *date*, and you keep access until that date.

Deleting your account also cancels the subscription. See [Delete account](/knowledge-base/settings/authentication#delete-account).
