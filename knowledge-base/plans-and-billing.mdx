## The plans

TryPost Cloud has two flat plans. Both include every feature — the difference is how many [workspaces](/knowledge-base/workspaces) you can run. There are no per-seat fees and no AI credit cap.

| | **Socials** | **Workspaces** |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | Creators and small brands | Agencies and larger businesses |
| Monthly | **$19 / month** | **$99 / month** |
| Yearly | **$15.83 / month** billed yearly (**$190 / year**, 2 months free) | **$82.50 / month** billed yearly (**$990 / year**, 2 months free) |
| Workspaces | 1 | Unlimited |
| Social accounts | Unlimited, including several on the same network | Unlimited, including several on the same network |
| Team members | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Included | Calendar, Copilot, MCP, REST API, analytics, [repurpose](/knowledge-base/repurposes), signatures, labels | Same |

Pricing is localized in some regions (for example, Brazil). See the [pricing page](https://trypost.it/pricing) for current rates.

A workspace is one brand or client, kept separate from the rest: its own social accounts, signatures, labels, analytics, members, and MCP connection. Need another brand? [Add a workspace](/knowledge-base/workspaces#creating-a-workspace) — Socials can hold one; Workspaces can hold as many as you need.

## First month

New accounts pick a plan during welcome. Checkout is **monthly** there. A card is required.

First-time subscribers pay **$1 for the first month**. The regular price — **$19** on Socials or **$99** on Workspaces — starts on the next cycle. Cancel before that cycle if you do not want to continue.

Re-subscribers do not get the $1 first month. Yearly billing is available later on the billing page — it is not part of welcome checkout.

If you let access lapse without an active subscription, the app and API return `402 Payment Required` until you subscribe.

## Subscribing

1. During welcome, pick **Socials** or **Workspaces** and complete Stripe Checkout
2. After you have a subscription, go to **Settings &gt; Account &gt; Billing** to switch plan or interval
3. Pick **Monthly** or **Yearly** at the top of the plan picker
4. Choose **Upgrade**, **Downgrade**, or switch interval

Yearly billing costs less per month than monthly (2 months free). Stripe prorates the difference when you change plan or interval.

## Changing plan

Use **Settings &gt; Account &gt; Billing**. Only the account owner can change the plan.

- **Socials → Workspaces** — upgrade. You can add more workspaces right away.
- **Workspaces → Socials** — downgrade. You must be down to **one** workspace first. If you still have extras, delete them before switching — the picker blocks Socials until you do.
- **Monthly ↔ yearly** on the same plan — switch interval from the same picker.

Adding a workspace on Workspaces does not change what you pay.

## Updating payment method &amp; invoices

Click **Manage on Stripe** on the Billing page to open the Stripe Customer Portal in a new tab. From there you can:

- Update card / payment method
- Download invoices and receipts
- Change billing email
- Cancel the subscription

## Limit behavior

The Cloud limits that still apply:

- **No active subscription** — the dashboard, API, and MCP return `402 Payment Required`
- **Socials workspace cap** — you cannot create a second workspace until you upgrade. The create page still opens; submitting it shows the upgrade dialog
- **Downgrade with too many workspaces** — blocked until extras are deleted

There is **no** monthly AI credit ceiling. Copilot and the other AI tools stay available on both plans.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How much does TryPost cost?">
    Two plans: Socials at $19/month or $190/year, and Workspaces at $99/month or $990/year. First-time subscribers pay $1 for the first month. Both include every feature. The only difference is one workspace vs unlimited workspaces.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I manage more than one brand?">
    Yes. Create a workspace per brand or client. Socials includes one workspace; Workspaces includes as many as you need, at the same flat price.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I pay per team member?">
    No. Members are unlimited on both plans.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are AI features metered?">
    No. There is no credit cap and nothing is deducted from a monthly quota. You need an active subscription (Cloud) or a configured AI provider (self-hosted).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if my card fails?">
    Stripe retries automatically. After the grace period, the subscription enters `past_due` and the dashboard shows a banner. New actions that require app access return `402` until billing recovers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I cancel any time?">
    Yes. Cancel from the Stripe Customer Portal. The subscription stays active until the end of the current billing period, then expires.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does self-hosted have plans?">
    No. Self-hosted instances bypass all subscription checks. You manage your own infrastructure costs.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
